מי בתמונות: מסך מתן שמות לפרצופים בטיול חדש

A new trip has nobody to compare against, so nothing can be named
automatically. This adds the missing first step: find the faces, group the
ones that look like the same person, ask the owner once, and from then on
name that person everywhere - in the photos already on the site and in every
photo that arrives later.

pipeline/facescan.py   finds faces, saves a crop and the 128 numbers per face,
                       groups the unnamed ones, reads back the owner's answers,
                       and rewrites people-tags.json for the whole trip
.github/workflows      installs prebuilt dlib, caches the models once, runs the
                       face step after every ingest; it can never fail the run
src/components/Faces   the "who is this?" screen: crops, the trip's people,
                       a box for a new name, "not now", "not someone I know"
src/pages/Trip         a new tab, members only
src/live.js            faceNames: one document per group, so two people can
                       name faces at the same time
firestore.rules        read for the pipeline, write for members of the trip

Nothing is ever guessed before the owner has given a name, and a name is only
carried over to a new face when the match is close and clearly better than the
runner-up. Everything written stays a suggestion - the tagging panel wins.
